Steady Steps

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Steady Steps, an Android habit-tracking app, handles information. Steady Steps is designed to work locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not operate a server for storing your habit data.

Habit data stored on your device

Habit names, goals, schedules, completion history, streaks, achievements, reminders, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Steady Steps does not upload this habit data to developer-operated servers or a cloud service.

You may export a JSON backup or a CSV report yourself. Those files are created only when you choose an export destination and remain where you save them until you delete them. Restoring a backup replaces the habit data currently stored in the app.

Android device-to-device transfer may move local Steady Steps data when you set up or transfer to another device. Cloud backup of the app's local data is disabled.

Advertising and your choices

The free version of Steady Steps displays banner advertising through Google AdMob. Depending on your region, your choices, and the ads available, Google and its advertising partners may process device and account identifiers, IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Where required, Steady Steps presents a consent message before requesting ads. If available for your region, you can review or change advertising choices from Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google describes its advertising and privacy practices in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Steady Steps offers an optional one-time purchase to remove advertising. Purchases are processed by Google Play. Steady Steps does not receive or store your payment-card number. Google handles purchase and payment records under its own policies.

Notifications

If you enable a reminder, Steady Steps schedules a local notification on your device. The app requests notification permission only when reminders are enabled. Reminder schedules are stored locally and may be rescheduled after a device restart.

Data retention and deletion

Your local habit data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Steady Steps. You control and delete any exported files through the location where you saved them. Because Steady Steps has no user accounts and does not store habit data on developer-operated servers, there is no developer-held habit account to delete.

Security

Steady Steps keeps habit data in local app storage. Advertising and Google Play purchase services use their own secure network services. The Google Mobile Ads SDK states that the data it collects is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Children

Steady Steps is not designed or marketed specifically for children. If the app's target audience is changed, this policy and its advertising configuration will be reviewed before that release.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices, advertising configuration, or legal obligations change. The current version will be posted at this address with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact method shown on the Steady Steps listing in Google Play.

Your choices: You can delete local app data, manage notification permission in Android settings, manage your Android advertising ID in Android settings, and use the in-app advertising privacy choices when that option is available.
